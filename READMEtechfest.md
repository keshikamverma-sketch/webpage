# Beyond the Known – Techfest, IIT Bombay

A futuristic landing page designed for Techfest, IIT Bombay, based on the concept **"Beyond the Known"**.

## Project Overview
The website captures the spirit of innovation, technology, competition and exploration through a modern digital experience.

## Key Sections
- **Compete – Enter the Arena:** Technical competitions and challenges.
- **Discover – Meet Tomorrow:** Emerging technologies and innovation.
- **Experience – After Dark, Beyond:** Entertainment and immersive experiences.

## Design Approach
A futuristic visual direction with modern typography, structured content and intuitive navigation.
